How to Build an AI Voice Agent for Your Business

Why businesses need an AI receptionist

Small businesses can miss calls during busy hours, lunch, after close and weekends. Before buying an AI receptionist, check your own call log and estimate the value of genuinely missed opportunities. A well-configured voice agent can answer routine questions such as hours, prices, location, availability and order status, while handing sensitive or unusual calls to a person.

This isn't a toy. We built one end-to-end and it greets customers by name, checks their order status from the CRM, and confirms deliveries in natural Hinglish — all autonomously.

The 4-part stack (and what it costs)

ComponentWhat it doesFree options
1. Speech-to-textTurns the caller's voice into textWhisper (local, free), Google STT free tier
2. The "brain" (LLM)Understands intent, checks your data, decides the replyDeepSeek, Gemini, Groq free tiers
3. Text-to-speechSpeaks the reply naturallyEdge TTS (free), Piper (free, offline)
4. TelephonyConnects to a real phone numberTwilio trial credit; WhatsApp Calling API (inbound free)

Stack total: $0–$100/month depending on call volume. Compare that to a receptionist's salary — the math is absurd in the business's favor.

What a call looks like end-to-end

  1. Customer calls → the system picks up in under a second.
  2. Voice → text (Whisper), text → the AI brain with your business knowledge (FAQ, prices, order DB).
  3. AI decides: answer directly (hours, order status), take a booking, or hand off to a human ("connecting you now").
  4. Reply → natural voice → back to the caller. Every call logged with a transcript.

The secret sauce is a small set of hard rules layered on top of the AI: if the caller says "human" or "agent," always hand off; if they say goodbye words, end politely. This kills the classic failure mode where the AI improvises instead of transferring.

Where it pays for itself

  • 🏥 Clinics: appointment bookings + reminders. One recovered booking a day covers the monthly cost.
  • 🏠 Real estate: instant response to listing enquiries (speed decides who gets the viewing).
  • 🛒 E-commerce: "Where's my order?" calls — 24/7 order status without a support team.
  • 🍔 Restaurants/salons: bookings, wait times, FAQs.
Rule of thumb: if a business misses 5+ calls a day, an AI voice agent pays for itself within the first month.

Pitfalls to avoid

  • ⚠️ Robot-sounding voices: test the TTS voice with real customers; a bad voice kills trust fast.
  • ⚠️ No hand-off path: always route "human" requests to a real person, always.
  • ⚠️ Cold generic responses: the agent must know YOUR prices, hours and orders — train it on real data.
  • ⚠️ Recording without consent: check call-recording laws in your region; announce recording or skip it.
