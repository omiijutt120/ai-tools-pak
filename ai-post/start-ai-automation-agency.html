How to Start an AI Automation Agency: The 4-Week Playbook

📅 2026-08-05 · · By Muhammad Umar

Why automation sells itself

Every small business does repetitive work: copying data, sending the same emails, chasing leads. They know it wastes hours; they just don't know how to fix it. You are selling hours back — and when you say this saves your team 10 hours a week, price stops being the objection.

The free tool stack

  • n8n (self-hosted) — workflow automation, community edition free
  • Make / Zapier — free tiers for simple triggers
  • Google Apps Script — free Sheets/Gmail automation
  • LLM free tiers (DeepSeek, Gemini, Groq) — the brain
  • WhatsApp Business API / Telegram — notifications and bot interfaces

The one offer that works

Niches that say yes fastest: clinics (appointment reminders), e-commerce (order tracking), real estate (lead follow-up), service businesses (booking).

The 4-week launch

  1. Week 1: build 3 mini-automations for yourself — email auto-responder, Sheets to WhatsApp alert, lead scraper to CRM. Demo reel done.
  2. Week 2: list 50 businesses in one niche; send 10 personalized messages a day. 'I noticed you reply to enquiries manually…'
  3. Week 3: demo calls. Ask their most annoying weekly task, then show exactly that automated.
  4. Week 4: close at starter price, deliver fast, then pitch the retainer: 'I'll maintain it and add one automation a month.'

Pricing without underselling

Starter$199–$499One workflow, 7-day delivery, handover docs

If a project saves 10 hours a week, your $500 fee pays for itself in a month. Quote with confidence — the math is on your side.
