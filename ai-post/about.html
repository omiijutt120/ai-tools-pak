About The AI Post

The AI Post is an independent publication covering artificial intelligence: daily news, hands-on tutorials, honest comparisons, tool reviews, free resources and prompt libraries.

Who writes it

Our team builds AI systems for real businesses — voice agents, chatbots, automations — and writes about what we learn in the field. We are not a course seller and we do not publish AI-generated spam.

What we publish

  • Tutorials — step-by-step guides that assume a $0 budget
  • Comparisons & reviews — hands-on, with costs and caveats
  • Free AI tools & prompt libraries — practical, tested

Editorial standards

All articles are original. News items are based on verifiable sources and carry publication dates. Corrections: email us and we fix errors promptly (see our Editorial Policy).
