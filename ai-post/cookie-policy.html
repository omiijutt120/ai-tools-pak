Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device that let websites remember preferences and measure usage.

Cookies we use

  • Essential: dark mode preference, bookmarks (stored locally in your browser).
  • Analytics/advertising: if advertising is enabled, Google AdSense may set cookies to personalize ads.
  • Preferences: theme and settings stored in your browser's local storage.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking may affect site features.

More info
