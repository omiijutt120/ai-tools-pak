13 Free AI Tools That Are Actually Free in 2026 (No Card Required)

📅 2026-08-07 · · By The AI Post Staff

How we define free

A tool made this list only if it passed all three tests: no credit card at signup, a free allowance that is actually useful for real work, and no silent auto-charge when the trial ends. We re-checked every entry in August 2026 — but free tiers change often, so verify before you build a business on any of them.

The fake-free traps to skip

  • 'Free trial' that silently converts to a paid plan — always check the billing page twice.
  • 'Free forever' that requires a call with a salesperson to actually get access.
  • Tools that gate the only useful feature behind a paywall and call the rest free.
  • Credit-gated APIs: if a card is required at signup, it is not a free tier.

Build a $0 AI stack from this list

  1. Brain: DeepSeek or Groq free tier for the LLM.
  2. Voice in: run Whisper locally for speech-to-text.
  3. Voice out: Edge TTS for natural replies.
  4. Orchestration: n8n community edition to connect everything.
  5. Storage: Google Sheets for logs and knowledge.

That is a complete AI voice agent — speech in, thinking, speech out — for zero model cost. We run exactly this pattern in production; the only bill is the server you already have.

Free tiers are not charity; they are the top of the funnel. Use them like a professional: build, validate, and only pay when volume genuinely demands it.
