WhatsApp Launches Calling API for Businesses: Inbound Calls Free, AI Agents Ready

📅 2026-08-06 · · By The AI Post Staff
📞

What happened

Meta has rolled out the WhatsApp Business Calling API, giving businesses full programmatic control over phone calls on WhatsApp. The documentation now covers business-initiated calls, user-initiated calls, call recording, and call transcription.

The important part for small businesses: a customer who calls your WhatsApp number can be answered automatically by software. No human needed on the other end — an AI agent can pick up, answer questions, take bookings, and confirm orders.

How the pricing works

  • User-initiated calls (customers calling you): FREE — no per-minute charge.
  • Business-initiated calls (you calling customers): paid per minute, billed in 6-second increments, with volume-tiered pricing by country.
  • Call transcription and recording: currently free on both call types.
  • Outbound calling requires a valid payment method on the account.

What this means for AI voice agents

Until now, building an AI receptionist meant routing through telephony providers. With the Calling API, a business's own WhatsApp number becomes the phone line. The flow works via WebRTC: the call arrives as an SDP offer, your server accepts it, and audio streams straight to your AI stack (speech-to-text, LLM, text-to-speech).

This is the missing piece for AI receptionist services on WhatsApp: inbound calls cost nothing, and the bot runs on your existing infrastructure.

What's still required

  • The business phone number must be registered on the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).
  • The calls webhook must be subscribed and the Calling API enabled on the number.
  • App Review is needed for the WhatsApp Business management and messaging permissions.
  • Callers must grant call permission for business-initiated calls.
Takeaway: inbound WhatsApp calls are now free and automatable — the cheapest possible phone line an AI agent can answer.
