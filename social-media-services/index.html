FAQ

Before ordering social media services.

Are prices final on this page?

Prices are imported from the latest PKR catalog file. Please confirm availability, quantity and final order details on WhatsApp before payment.

Do you collect account passwords?

No. The website does not collect passwords or two-factor authentication codes. Sensitive services are handled manually through support.

Can I search every service?

Yes. Search runs across the full local catalog before pagination is applied.

Do SMM services guarantee real customers?

No. Followers, likes and views are visible metrics and do not guarantee customers, sales, loyal audiences or long-term reach.

Can an order affect my account?

Third-party engagement may conflict with platform rules. Activity can be removed and platforms may restrict accounts, so understand the risk first.

What information should I send?

Send only the required public profile, post or video link. Never send a password, recovery code or two-factor authentication code.

What should I measure after an order?

Track meaningful outcomes such as enquiries, website visits, watch quality and sales separately from the purchased metric.
