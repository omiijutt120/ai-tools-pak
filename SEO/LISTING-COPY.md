## Directory description

AI Tools Pak helps Pakistani students, freelancers, creators and small teams compare AI subscription options in PKR before they contact support. Each catalog page states the listed price, plan duration, access model and buyer-safety checks. The site also publishes a transparent AI Subscription Price Index Pakistan, with scope, methodology and product-source links for writers and researchers.
