## Content and experience gaps

### P0/P1 opportunities

1. Publish one factual order-process page covering WhatsApp confirmation, accepted payment methods, delivery checkpoints, access model and refund-condition checks.
2. Add workflow hubs: AI assistants, video, voice, writing/SEO, design and creator marketing. Each hub should link to its products, comparisons and supporting guides.
3. Strengthen comparison discovery. The seven comparison pages existed but their hub was orphaned on the live crawl; the local navigation and breadcrumb fix now routes authority to it.
4. Add an evidence-led “private vs shared vs team access” explainer. Avoid legal or official-reseller claims unless reviewed and documented.
5. Expand genuine-account verification and troubleshooting guidance using only checks the business actually performs.

### Differentiation already present

- PKR price, duration and access model are explicit on generated product pages.
- Buyer-safety language avoids claiming official authorization without evidence.
- `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `agents.md`, XML sitemap and structured product data give strong machine-readable coverage.
- Product pages are deeper and more Pakistan-specific than bundle-only competitors.

## Four-week execution sequence

1. Week 1: deploy/verify homepage 32-product schema parity; expose comparison and additional-guide hubs; submit updated sitemap.
2. Week 2: ship ordering/access-model hub and connect it from every commercial template.
3. Week 3: publish three workflow hubs (assistants, video, writing/SEO) and consolidate competing internal anchors around designated pages.
4. Week 4: publish factual payment/troubleshooting content, then measure GSC query/page pairs and conversions before expanding.
