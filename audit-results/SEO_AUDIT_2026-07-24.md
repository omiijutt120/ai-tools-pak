# SEO, GEO and AEO audit — 24 July 2026

## Scope and outcome

Audit scope: `https://aitoolspak.tech/`, its published sitemap, local deploy source and the visible search presence of DigitalTools and AI Wala. No product data, prices, listing order or visual design was changed.

The site has a solid technical base: 43 canonical sitemap URLs, unique metadata and a valid structured-data implementation. All 43 published sitemap URLs returned HTTP 200 during the live check. The following discovery files also returned HTTP 200: `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `feed.xml`, `og-image.png` and `favicon.ico`.

## What is already working

- Pakistan-specific commercial intent is clear: PKR price, duration, access model, WhatsApp support and buyer-safety guidance.
- The site has dedicated product guides, an editorial blog hub, commercial buying guides and platform-specific SMM landing pages instead of exposing supplier-row pages to search.
- Canonicals, titles, descriptions, visible H1s, JSON-LD and internal links pass the project audit for all 43 sitemap URLs.
- Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Product, Article, Breadcrumb and visible-FAQ markup are present where appropriate.
- `robots.txt`, the XML sitemap, RSS feed and LLM-oriented discovery files are indexable and reachable.

## Guardrails

- Ranking cannot be guaranteed; search performance depends on crawl/indexing, competition, trust and user response.
- Do not update prices, durations, stock claims or `dateModified` values without a real verification.
- Do not create thin pages for each social-service supplier row. Keep a small number of useful editorial landing pages.
- Keep all product claims aligned with the visible plan, refund, delivery and warranty terms.
