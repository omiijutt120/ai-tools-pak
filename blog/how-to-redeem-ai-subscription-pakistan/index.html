How to Redeem Your AI Subscription in Pakistan

Ordered a tool and not sure what happens next? Here is exactly how redemption and delivery work for AI subscriptions in Pakistan.

Published: August 4, 2026. Last updated: August 4, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

What happens after you order

Once you order an AI subscription via WhatsApp and confirm payment, we activate the plan on your own account — never on a shared account and never using your personal password. Delivery is typically completed within hours. This guide explains exactly how redemption works so you know what to expect.

How redemption and activation work

Redemption is the point where your purchased plan becomes live on your account. Here is the standard flow:

  • 1. Order from your product page, for example ChatGPT Plus or Gemini Pro.
  • 2. Confirm the PKR price and send payment details via WhatsApp.
  • 3. Once payment is confirmed, we activate the plan on your own account.
  • 4. You receive confirmation with access details and usage instructions.
  • 5. If anything is unclear, message us — support is via WhatsApp and support@aitoolspak.com.

Delivery expectations

Most subscriptions are activated and delivered within hours of payment confirmation, though popular plans can take a little longer during high demand. If there is any delay, we will keep you updated in WhatsApp. You can read the full expectations in our delivery policy.

If a problem occurs and delivery cannot be completed, our refund policy explains how you are covered. We stand behind every order.

Keep your account safe after redemption

Once your plan is active, keep the account in your own name and do not share your password. Never buy from sellers who ask for your password or sell shared accounts. For more, see our safe subscription guide and free vs paid tools guide.
