What the Team plan adds

If one ChatGPT Plus seat at PKR 1833 is not enough, the ChatGPT Team plan is the next step up. It is built for businesses, agencies, and freelance teams that need a shared, managed workspace with higher usage limits than an individual Plus seat.

  • A dedicated team workspace separate from personal chats
  • Higher usage caps than the individual Plus plan
  • Admin controls to manage seats and permissions
  • No training on your business data

Who should get it

Team plans suit agencies running client work, small offices sharing an assistant, and freelance groups that want one central account instead of everyone juggling personal logins. If you just use AI for your own tasks, a single Plus seat is usually the cheaper, smarter route.

Because the Team plan includes everything in Plus — the higher-quality model, faster responses, and image generation — it is really a question of whether you need the shared workspace and admin features.
