What Jasper does for copywriters and marketers

Jasper is a brand-voice copywriting tool. It is designed to write marketing copy, blog posts, ad creatives, emails and social captions in a consistent brand voice, with SEO features for content teams.

  • Brand voice: keep your tone consistent across every draft
  • SEO content: briefs, outlines and long-form articles built around keywords
  • Campaign copy: ad variants, landing pages and email sequences in minutes
  • Templates and workflows for agencies producing content at scale
