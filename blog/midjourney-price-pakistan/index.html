Midjourney official price in USD and PKR

Midjourney sells monthly subscriptions directly on midjourney.com. The official list prices are USD 10 (Basic), USD 30 (Standard), USD 60 (Pro) and USD 120 (Mega) per month. The PKR figures below are approximations at the ~PKR 280/USD rate — the exact amount you pay moves with the exchange rate, so always confirm the current PKR total before paying.

What each Midjourney plan includes

Every paid plan includes access to Midjourney's image generator through the web editor and Discord. The tiers differ in monthly generation allowance and queue speed, so pick based on volume rather than price alone.

  • Basic: a limited number of fast generations per month with a slower queue — enough to test the tool properly
  • Standard: the most popular tier, with more fast GPU time plus unlimited relaxed-mode generations
  • Pro: significantly more fast GPU time, plus stealth mode for private generations
  • Mega: the largest allowance for high-volume production, aimed at studios and heavy creators
