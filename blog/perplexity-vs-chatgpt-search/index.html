Perplexity vs ChatGPT Search: Which AI Search Engine for Pakistan?

Perplexity and ChatGPT Search both answer questions with live web sources, but they are built differently. This guide compares citations, follow-up research and what each costs in PKR.

Published: August 6, 2026. Last updated: August 6, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

Two ways to search with AI

Perplexity is a search engine first: every answer comes with numbered sources you can open and check. ChatGPT Search is a feature inside ChatGPT: you get an answer, then a set of source links, all inside the same chat where you can keep asking follow-ups.

Both are free to start. Perplexity's free tier gives a limited number of searches per day, while ChatGPT free includes browsing with usage caps. Paid plans cost the same: Perplexity Pro is officially USD 20 per month and ChatGPT Plus is officially USD 20 per month, both around PKR 5,600 at the approximate PKR 280 rate.

Citation quality

Perplexity is the stronger citator. Its answers are built around sources, with inline numbers next to every claim and a clean source panel on the side. That structure is a lifesaver for university assignments, research proposals and any work where you must show where information came from.

  • Perplexity: numbered inline citations, a source panel, and follow-up questions that stay grounded in the cited pages
  • ChatGPT Search: sources shown as links under the answer, better for conversational exploration than formal citation

If your supervisor or client asks where a claim came from, Perplexity answers faster and more convincingly.

Follow-up research and depth

Perplexity shines at iterative research: explain a topic, compare two options, then ask what changed last year. Each turn stays grounded in fresh web results. Pro adds file uploads, more search credits and access to multiple frontier models for the same query.

ChatGPT Search is better when the answer feeds into a deliverable. You research, then immediately ask for a summary, an email, a report or a spreadsheet in the same thread. For freelancers, that research-to-output flow saves real time.
