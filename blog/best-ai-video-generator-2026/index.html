Veo 3 — the quality benchmark

Google's Veo 3 is the current benchmark for realistic AI video. It generates consistent characters, natural motion and synchronized audio, which makes it ideal for real-estate tours, product ads and anything meant to look professionally shot. Start with standard Veo 3 and scale to Veo 3 Ultra (PKR 7,083) or the Extension add-on (PKR 2,500) as your volume grows.

Runway ML — best for editors

If editing control matters more than raw generation, Runway ML is your tool. Its motion-brush, inpainting and timeline-friendly output suit creators who build from a script and refine shot by shot. At PKR 2,100 it is also the most wallet-friendly of the premium tier.

HeyGen — AI presenters and talking heads

Need a presenter who never gets tired? HeyGen creates studio-quality avatar videos, lip-synced multilingual presentations and talking-head ads. It is the go-to for YouTube educational channels, corporate explainers and localization work. At PKR 6,167, it is priced for serious video operators rather than casual testers.

Hailuo AI — the budget pick

Hailuo AI at PKR 1,700 is the best entry-level buy. It produces surprisingly strong clips for social content without the premium price tag, making it perfect for freelancers testing the AI-video waters or scaling up multiple client accounts on a budget.
