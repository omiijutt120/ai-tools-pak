Netflix vs Prime Video in Pakistan — Which is Worth It in PKR?

Two streaming giants, two honest PKR prices: Netflix at 417 and Prime Video at 333. Here is how they compare.

Published: August 4, 2026. Last updated: August 4, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

The quick answer

Both Netflix and Prime Video are streaming subscriptions rather than AI tools, but we list them in PKR for convenience. Netflix (PKR 417) is the broader, more polished library. Prime Video (PKR 333) is more affordable and bundles with Amazon's ecosystem. The difference between them is about PKR 84 a month — so for many households, the honest answer is to consider both.

Which streaming service should you choose?

Pick Netflix if you want the largest, most consistent catalogue and watch with a family or group. Pick Prime Video if the price is the deciding factor or you already use Amazon. And if you watch a lot, buying both keeps you under PKR 750 a month for two substantial libraries.
