Pick SuperGrok (PKR 2083) if...

You want a distinctive, opinionated assistant with strong access to real-time information and a style that stands out from the rest. At 2083 PKR it sits comfortably alongside the other two, and xAI's Grok subscription is a great pick if you are already in the X ecosystem.

Pick ChatGPT Plus (PKR 1833) if...

You want the safest, most versatile default. ChatGPT Plus at 1833 PKR is the assistant most students and freelancers reach for first, with the strongest all-round reasoning, writing, and coding.

Pick Gemini Pro (PKR 750) if...

Budget matters most. Gemini Pro at 750 PKR costs roughly a third as much as SuperGrok and still handles everyday questions, drafting, and Google-tool workflows well. It is the best entry point if you have never paid for an AI assistant.
