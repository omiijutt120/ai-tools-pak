OpenAI's Donut-Shaped AI Speaker Will Reportedly Cost $300 to $400 - and It Is Not Coming Until 2027

Donut-shaped premium metal smart speaker on a kitchen counter next to a ChatGPT logo

Published: August 8, 2026, 2026. News date: August 6, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

The news: OpenAI's mystery device gets a price tag

Bloomberg reports that OpenAI's long-rumored hardware device - previously described as an AI-fueled smart speaker and the physical manifestation of ChatGPT - will be donut-shaped, so users can carry it around the home from bedside table to kitchen counter.

Sources describe a device built from high-quality metal with a premium look and distinct moving parts, priced at $300 to $400 per unit. For scale, Amazon's smart speakers range from roughly $40 to $240.

The device is being developed with LoveFrom, the design studio of former Apple design chief Jony Ive, and is likely to be released sometime in 2027. The report does not say when pricing will be confirmed or how many units OpenAI plans, but the company has framed the hardware as the physical manifestation of ChatGPT - a constant presence at home rather than a productivity tool.

Why it matters: hardware is OpenAI's most visible bet yet

A $300 to $400 gadget is a different league from a budget Echo Dot. OpenAI is betting that a premium, tactile ChatGPT device - one you carry around the house, with moving parts you can feel - can justify a price four to ten times what consumers pay for smart speakers today.

The 2027 timeline matters just as much. Voice AI and hardware both move fast, and a device described this far in advance can easily change shape, slip, or get reworked before it ships. Treat every detail here as a plan, not a promise.

There is also a legal wrinkle. Apple is suing OpenAI over alleged trade secrets theft - a claim OpenAI denies - and if that case drags on, it could affect the hardware team's focus and the product's timing.

What it means for Pakistan
