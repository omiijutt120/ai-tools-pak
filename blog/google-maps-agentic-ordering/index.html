Google Maps Can Now Order Your Food, Book Your Hotel, and Find Event Tickets for You

Google Maps on a phone showing Ask Maps ordering food and booking a hotel with agentic controls

Published: August 8, 2026. News date: August 6, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

The news: Ask Maps grows hands

Google announced on August 6, 2026 that Ask Maps is gaining agentic capabilities: it can now order food, book hotels, and find event tickets on your behalf, moving from answering questions to completing real-world tasks. The announcement continues Google's push to make Maps a hub for everyday errands, not just navigation.

Food ordering starts with a natural request like 'Where can I order vegan avocado toast and an oat milk latte near home?' Ask Maps surfaces restaurants, and an Order Online action places the order through Square, Toast, or Uber Eats - items go into the cart and payment happens on the supported platform.

Hotels and events work the same way. Ask for a decently priced, top-rated hotel with an artsy vibe within walking distance of a conference, and Ask Maps compares prices and checks availability. Ask about comedy shows or live music near work, and it returns a list with ticket links. Google gave examples for all three, and each one ends with an action taken, not just a list shown.

Why it matters: this is what agentic AI looks like in the mainstream

Google is also rolling out Personal Intelligence, which answers questions about your upcoming flights, dinner reservations, and hotels by pulling from your emails - and it remembers prior conversations. A live transit widget with real-time delays ships alongside it.

Both Personal Intelligence and the live transit widget are rolling out to all markets where Ask Maps is available. The big shift is that a consumer app with billions of users is now executing tasks, not just returning results - and that changes how businesses need to think about being found.

This is also the clearest sign yet that agentic AI is not a developer niche. When Google Maps starts acting on your behalf, the everyday user has an agent in their pocket without ever installing a specialist tool.
