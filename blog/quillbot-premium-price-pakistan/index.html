Free vs Premium: what QuillBot Premium adds

The free version is useful for light paraphrasing and basic grammar help. Premium unlocks the full toolset that students and writers depend on. The difference is most visible on long documents, where the free limits force you to stop and restart constantly.

  • More paraphrase modes with better control over tone and formality
  • Higher word limits so long essays and papers can be processed in one go
  • Grammar checker and plagiarism checker for polished, original work
  • No ads and faster processing during busy study periods

Who QuillBot Premium suits in Pakistan

Students, teachers and academic writers are QuillBot's core users. Paraphrasing helps rework drafts, grammar checks clean up submissions, and the plagiarism checker is a final safety net before submission.

For students, the annual plan at about PKR 1,200 per month equivalent is the budget-friendly choice, while month-to-month makes sense for a short semester or a single project.

Teachers also use QuillBot to prepare worksheets, simplify texts for younger students and check submissions for originality before grading.
