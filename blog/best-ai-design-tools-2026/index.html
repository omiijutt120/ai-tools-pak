Which AI design tool should you pick?

Match the tool to the deliverable, not the hype.

  • Social media posts and posters: Canva Pro — fastest path from template to publishable design
  • Logos and brand marks: Midjourney or Ideogram — Ideogram wins when text has to be exact
  • Product shots and marketing visuals: Midjourney, then clean up in Canva
  • Game assets and stylized art: Leonardo AI
  • Quick inline images while writing: DALL-E inside ChatGPT Plus
