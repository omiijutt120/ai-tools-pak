Why API credit pricing works differently in Pakistan

When you call an AI model through an API, you are usually billed per token or per request — in dollars, against an overseas payment method. For a Pakistani developer that means unpredictable rupee costs, currency risk and the hassle of paying abroad. Enterprise AI API Credits solve this by packaging AI usage as credits you procure locally, quoted in PKR to fit your expected workload.

How Enterprise AI API Credits work

  • You tell us what you are building and roughly how much usage you expect.
  • We quote your credits in PKR, matched to your scale — from a small project to heavy production workload.
  • You order via WhatsApp and pay with a bank debit card or JazzCash.
  • Credits are applied to your account and you draw them down as your code makes API calls.

This pay-what-you-need model lets developers prototype cheaply and scale credits as their product grows. Enquire for a PKR quote or message us on WhatsApp.

Typical use cases for API credits
