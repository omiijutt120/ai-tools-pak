Netflix vs Prime Video — which should you pick?

They overlap but lean in different directions. Netflix (PKR 417) has a deep, polished catalogue of series, documentaries and original films. Many households buy both, since together they cost under PKR 750 a month — far less than a gym membership or a single family dinner.

Pay safely for streaming in Pakistan

Both services are billed in PKR through local ordering, payable via WhatsApp with a bank debit card or JazzCash, and activated on your own account within hours.
