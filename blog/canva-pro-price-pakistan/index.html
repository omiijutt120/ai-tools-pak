Canva Pro price in Pakistan: official price and PKR conversion

Canva Pro is officially priced at USD 12.99 per month (or USD 119.99 per year) on canva.com. At typical Pakistani exchange rates that is approximately PKR 3,600–3,800 per month. The exact PKR amount changes with the exchange rate, so always confirm the current payable amount in PKR before paying.

Prices from local Pakistani sellers often differ from the official canva.com price because of access method (personal account, team seat or activation), payment method and seller margins. A lower price is not a problem by itself — the problem is paying before the access model, duration and warranty are clear.

What Canva Pro includes

  • Unlimited premium design tools, magic resize and background remover
  • Brand Kit to store logos, colors and fonts
  • 1 TB of cloud storage and 100+ premium fonts
  • Priority support and 100,000+ premium templates
  • Canva Magic Studio AI features (Magic Write, Magic Edit and more)

Canva Free vs Canva Pro in Pakistan

Canva Free is enough for occasional designs: basic templates, limited storage and standard uploads. Canva Pro adds the premium tools, Brand Kit, background remover and AI features that freelancers and small businesses need daily. If you design social posts, pitch decks, thumbnails or client creatives every week, Pro usually pays for itself.

What Pakistani buyers should ask

  • What is the exact current price in PKR?
  • Is the access private, shared, account-based or activation-based?
  • How long will the plan remain active?
  • What information is needed for activation?
  • What happens if activation cannot be completed?
  • How long does support remain available after delivery?

Safety checklist

Do not share your email password. Confirm whether the access is private, shared, account-based or activation-based. Ask what support is available after delivery. Keep order messages in WhatsApp so you have the confirmed price, duration and warranty in writing.

Also check whether the seller clearly says they are not an official partner unless they can prove authorization. Transparent wording is safer than exaggerated claims like lifetime unlimited access or official reseller status without evidence.
