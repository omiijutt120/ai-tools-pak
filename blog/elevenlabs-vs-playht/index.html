Voice quality and cloning

ElevenLabs is known for natural, expressive voices and high-quality voice cloning, which makes it a favourite for audiobooks, character voices and emotive ad reads. PlayHT produces clean, consistent narration voices that work well for explainer videos, IVR prompts and bulk voiceover jobs where you need reliable output fast.

For Pakistani freelancers, the practical question is whether the voice handles the mix of English and Urdu your clients need. Test both free plans with your own script before committing to a paid tier. Voice style matters too: a warm, slower voice suits audiobooks, while a crisp, faster one fits ads and explainers.

Urdu and English support

Both platforms handle English narration well and offer multilingual voices that include Urdu options. The real test is your own script: generate the same Urdu ad line or English explainer sentence in both tools and listen for pronunciation, pacing and naturalness.

  • ElevenLabs: expressive multilingual voices with strong cloning for character work
  • PlayHT: clean narration voices across many languages for steady production
