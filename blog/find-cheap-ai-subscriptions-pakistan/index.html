What "cheap" means for AI subscriptions in Pakistan

A low price only helps if the plan is actually delivered and supported. A shared account with no refund terms is a worse deal than a private plan with clear delivery and support hours. Compare the access model, delivery time, support hours and refund conditions, not just the PKR number.

How to buy a cheap AI subscription safely

  1. Pick the tool from the catalog and note its current PKR price and plan duration.
  2. Message the seller on WhatsApp and confirm price, duration, access model, delivery estimate and refund terms.
  3. Pay only after the seller explains the order clearly.
  4. Keep the WhatsApp chat as your order record until the support window ends.
  5. Never share your email password.
