OpenAI Acquires Presentation Startup NextSlide — the Team Now Works on ChatGPT

OpenAI has quietly absorbed NextSlide, a presentation AI startup whose founder previously sold a startup to Instacart. The team is now working on ChatGPT — and slide generation is clearly on the roadmap.

Published: August 9, 2026. News date: August 8, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

The news: OpenAI picks up NextSlide

OpenAI has acquired NextSlide, a presentation-focused AI startup, TechCrunch reported on August 8, 2026. The startup's website now carries a note from founder Ahmed Beshry announcing that the team has joined OpenAI and is working on ChatGPT. Financial terms were not disclosed, and Beshry said on LinkedIn that the acquisition actually closed 'earlier this year' — the announcement is just arriving 'a few months late.'

NextSlide's product could turn prompts, notes, documents or research into a polished, editable presentation. Beshry described the founding mission as 'making visual communication more accessible and helping more people express their ideas clearly' — a mission the team says it will continue inside OpenAI.

Beshry is no stranger to acquisitions: he previously co-founded Caper AI, the smart-cart checkout startup that Instacart bought in 2021. That background — building AI-powered hardware and consumer tools — suggests OpenAI is buying talent and product sensibility as much as code.

Why it matters: slides are a wedge into the office

Presentations are one of the most common professional tasks on earth — and one of the least loved. Every student, freelancer, agency and sales team in Pakistan produces decks weekly. If ChatGPT can turn a rough document into a genuinely editable, well-designed presentation, it removes a whole category of grunt work from the average knowledge worker's week.

The acquisition also signals where OpenAI is spending: not just on frontier models, but on the everyday productivity surface area around ChatGPT. Canvas, document editing and now presentations — OpenAI is assembling the pieces of a full office suite, the same territory Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 have dominated for two decades.

What it means for Pakistan

For Pakistani students preparing thesis defences and university presentations, and for freelancers who pitch clients on Upwork and Fiverr, deck-making is real billable time. A ChatGPT-native presentation feature could compress a two-hour design-and-format session into ten minutes of prompting and light editing. That is the kind of leverage that shows up directly in earnings for freelancers who bill by the deliverable.

There is a safety note worth repeating: when a new ChatGPT feature like slide generation arrives, verify it through official channels before trusting third-party tutorials — and never hand your ChatGPT or email password to a 'tool' that promises free presentation generation. Legitimate features ship inside the app you already pay for. The bigger picture

Talent acquisitions like this rarely produce features overnight — the NextSlide team will likely spend months integrating its technology into ChatGPT's existing canvas and document tools. But the direction is unmistakable: 2026's AI assistants are becoming creation suites, and the winners will be the ones who make professional output — slides, documents, spreadsheets — feel effortless.
