QuillBot Premium (PKR 399)

At just 399 PKR, QuillBot Premium is the budget champion. It shines at paraphrasing and rewriting, making it ideal for students and writers who want to rephrase drafts, fix grammar, and speed up editing without a big spend.

WordAI (PKR 500)

For content teams that need to spin large volumes of text while keeping it readable, WordAI at 500 PKR is the workhorse. It focuses on bulk rewriting with a higher quality of output than free spinners, a good match for agencies and content marketers.

Grammarly Premium (PKR 833)

If your priority is professional proofreading, tone, and clarity across emails, reports, and documents, Grammarly Premium at 833 PKR is the most polished pick. It is more about making your writing sound right than rewriting it wholesale.
