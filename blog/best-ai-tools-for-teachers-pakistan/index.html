The shortlist

Six tools cover nearly everything a teacher does, from planning to feedback. Start free, upgrade only where it saves real hours.

  • ChatGPT — lesson plans, quiz questions and model answers.
  • Gemini — worksheets plus Google Classroom integration.
  • DeepSeek — free generation of Urdu and English material.
  • Canva — visual worksheets, posters and presentation slides.
  • Grammarly Premium — clean grammar and feedback on student writing.
  • QuillBot — paraphrasing and summaries for comprehension work.

Lesson planning and worksheets

Give the chatbot your topic, class level and time slot, and ask for a plan with objectives, activities and homework.

  • ChatGPT and Gemini produce ready-to-edit lesson plans in minutes.
  • DeepSeek generates the same quality for free, with good Urdu/English switching for bilingual worksheets.
  • Always review output for syllabus fit — AI does not know your board's requirements.

Quiz generation and grading help

Ask for MCQs, fill-in-the-blanks or short questions with answer keys in a single prompt.

  • ChatGPT can generate a 20-question quiz with an answer key and difficulty levels.
  • The Gemini app lets you photograph a printed worksheet and turn it into digital questions.
  • For grading, use AI to draft feedback comments, then verify every mark yourself.
