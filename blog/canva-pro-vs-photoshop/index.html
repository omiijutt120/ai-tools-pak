Different tools, different jobs

Comparing Canva to Photoshop is a bit like comparing a modern kitchen to a professional test kitchen. Canva is the fastest route from idea to a polished social post; Photoshop is the depth option for photographers and detailed compositing. The right answer depends on your output.

For Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, flyers, resumes and brand kits, Canva Pro is unbeatable for speed.
