DeepSeek vs ChatGPT: Free AI Chatbot Comparison for Pakistan (2026)

DeepSeek's chat is officially free and its API is among the cheapest around, while ChatGPT offers a solid free tier and Plus at USD 20 per month. Here is how they compare for Pakistani students and freelancers.

Published: August 6, 2026. Last updated: August 6, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

The price difference is the headline

DeepSeek's web and app chat is officially free with no subscription, which makes it the default choice for anyone in Pakistan who wants capable AI at zero cost. ChatGPT also has a free tier, but the best model experience sits behind ChatGPT Plus at the official USD 20 per month (approximately PKR 5,600 at the approximate PKR 280 rate).

For a student or a freelancer just starting out, PKR 5,600 per month is a real decision. DeepSeek removes the decision entirely: sign up, chat, done.

Free-tier limits compared

ChatGPT's free tier is generous but capped: GPT-5 usage is limited, and image generation and advanced features are reserved mostly for Plus. DeepSeek's free chat does not meter you the same way, so heavy everyday use stays free.

  • DeepSeek free: unlimited-feeling chat, file uploads and a web search toggle with no daily quota pressure
  • ChatGPT free: solid GPT-5 access with usage caps, browsing and limited file uploads
  • ChatGPT Plus (USD 20/month): higher limits, DALL·E 3 images, advanced voice and tools

Reasoning, coding and Urdu support

DeepSeek's reasoning mode is genuinely strong at math, logic and coding tasks, which is why it became so popular with developers. ChatGPT is the more consistent all-rounder: slightly better at creative writing, structured output and following long instructions.

Both handle Urdu and Roman Urdu well for chat and translation. DeepSeek tends to be more literal, while ChatGPT produces more polished Urdu marketing copy and emails. For coding, both can write and debug; DeepSeek wins on price per query, ChatGPT on reliability across many project types.

DeepSeek API: the developer option

If you are building a bot or automating client work, DeepSeek's official API pricing is hard to beat. DeepSeek-chat costs USD 0.27 per 1M input tokens (cache miss), USD 0.07 per 1M on cache hit, and USD 1.10 per 1M output tokens. The deepseek-reasoner model costs USD 0.55 per 1M input (cache miss), USD 0.14 on cache hit, and USD 2.19 per 1M output. At the approximate PKR 280 rate, that is roughly PKR 76 per 1M input tokens for chat.

ChatGPT's API has its own pricing on OpenAI's site; the point is that DeepSeek lets developers prototype for pennies. If you do not want to manage a foreign payment method, you can buy API credits in Pakistan from a local supplier instead.

Speed and reliability from Pakistan

DeepSeek's servers are based in China, and while access from Pakistan generally works, response speed can dip during peak hours in Asia. ChatGPT's infrastructure is generally more consistent, and the paid tier gets priority during busy periods.

For exam nights and deadline mornings, reliability matters. If your work depends on the chatbot being up, keep a free ChatGPT account as a backup even if DeepSeek is your daily driver.

Which fits your budget?

Budget-conscious students: DeepSeek, no question. It is free, strong at reasoning and good enough for assignments, summaries and Urdu translation. Freelancers earning in dollars should consider ChatGPT Plus once free limits slow them down, because GPT-5 plus image generation pays for itself on a single client order.
