Helium 10 Free vs Paid Tiers: Which Plan Should Pakistani Sellers Buy?

Helium 10 grows with you from a free tier to Platinum and Diamond — here is exactly what each unlocks and which plan suits your Amazon business in PKR.

Published: August 4, 2026. Last updated: August 4, 2026. Reviewed by AI Tools Pak Editorial.

Start free, then level up

Helium 10 is Amazon sellers' favourite suite for product research, keyword tracking and listing optimisation. Its free plan is a genuine on-ramp — enough to test the workflow. As your catalogue and revenue grow, the paid tiers add the data volume and tools that serious selling demands.

The free tier

  • Limited daily searches and keyword lookups
  • Core product research tools with capped usage
  • Access to basic listing optimisation features
  • Good for first-time sellers testing the waters

Helium 10 Platinum

Helium 10 Platinum is the most popular paid tier — the natural upgrade for active sellers. At PKR 2,083 it unlocks generous search limits, full keyword research, competitor tracking and the suite's flagship features at a price that fits a growing business.

Helium 10 Diamond

Helium 10 Diamond sits at PKR 3,750 and adds the highest usage limits, advanced tooling and priority features for established sellers and teams running multiple products or stores. Pay the premium only when you are actually hitting Platinum's caps.
