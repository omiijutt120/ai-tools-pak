Here is the honest headline: ChatGPT Plus at PKR 1833 and Gemini Pro at PKR 750 are both budget AI. Neither costs hundreds of dollars. Compared to the cost of a single course, a cinema trip, or a meal out, these are affordable monthly tools for anyone in Pakistan.

When to pick ChatGPT Plus

Pick Plus when you need the strongest overall reasoning for coding, long writing tasks, or detailed research. The 1833 PKR outlay buys the most versatile assistant on the market, and it is the safest all-round default for students and freelancers.

When to pick Gemini Pro

Pick Gemini when budget is the first concern or you simply want a fast, reliable assistant tightly connected to Google tools. At 750 PKR it is one of the cheapest paid AI plans you can buy, making it a near-zero-risk entry point.
