The two subscriptions at a glance

ChatGPT Plus gives you GPT-5 with image generation, browsing and file uploads at the official USD 20 per month (about PKR 5,600). Claude Pro gives you Claude Sonnet and Opus at the official USD 20 per month, and on AI Tools Pak it is priced at a flat PKR 4,583 with local payment.

Same dollar price, different strengths. GPT-5 is the generalist; Claude Sonnet is the specialist in long-form writing and complex coding.

Writing quality

Claude Sonnet is widely considered one of the best pure writers among the major models: natural prose, strong structure and fewer AI-sounding phrases. For Pakistani freelancers writing client reports, proposals, blog posts and cold emails in English, Claude consistently needs the least editing.

GPT-5 is close behind and more versatile: it can switch from a formal proposal to a WhatsApp-style caption, then generate the accompanying image. If your work mixes writing with images, research and brainstorming, GPT-5 covers more ground in one subscription.

Coding and long-context work

Both are excellent at coding. Claude Sonnet is known for clean, well-structured code and careful refactoring, with a large context window that suits reviewing big codebases. GPT-5 is also strong, with broad tooling and integrations that make it convenient inside workflows.

For long documents, Claude's large context window lets you paste an entire thesis, contract or codebase and work on it coherently. GPT-5 handles long sessions well too, and adds image inputs and DALL·E 3, which Claude does not have.
