FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

Can the AI agent speak Urdu?

Yes. The agent speaks Urdu and English and can switch between them on the same call. The voice is natural and uses your business name and tone.

What happens if the AI cannot answer a question?

You define the fallback: the agent can politely take the caller's name and number and send you an instant WhatsApp alert, or transfer the call to your number during your working hours.

Do I need new phones, SIMs or hardware?

No. The agent works with your existing phone number. We handle the setup on our side and you keep full control of your number.

How fast can we go live?

Most projects go live in 5-7 working days after you share your FAQs and scripts. Simple WhatsApp bots can be live in 2-3 days.

What is included in the monthly fee?

The monthly fee covers hosting, the voice/AI service usage for your traffic, small content updates and support. Larger changes are quoted separately.

How do we order or get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp with your business type. We reply with a clear scope and PKR quote - nothing is charged until you approve the scope.
