# AI Tools Pak — Master SEO, GEO and AEO Strategy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

## 1. Business objective

Build topical authority and commercial visibility for Pakistan-focused searches such as **cheap AI tools Pakistan**, **AI tools sale**, **AI tools Pakistan**, **ChatGPT Plus price in Pakistan**, and **cheap SMM services Pakistan**. The site should convert qualified visitors through clear PKR pricing, written order terms and WhatsApp support. No SEO plan can guarantee the top position; the goal is sustained growth in relevant impressions, clicks, leads and orders.

## 2. Positioning

AI Tools Pak should be described consistently as a Pakistan-focused comparison, catalog and buying-guide website for AI subscriptions and social media services. Every page should reinforce these trust points:

- PKR pricing and Pakistan-specific buyer context.
- Clear duration, access model, delivery, warranty and refund checks.
- No email password or two-factor code requests.
- No unsupported claim of official partnership, lifetime access or guaranteed results.
- Product names and trademarks remain the property of their owners.

## 4. On-page rules

- One unique title, description, H1 and canonical per URL.
- Put the direct answer within the first 100–150 words.
- Use PKR, Pakistan, duration, access model and buyer checks naturally.
- Add a visible FAQ section based on real buyer questions. FAQPage markup is used as semantic structured data; it does not guarantee a Google rich result.
- Link each article to relevant product pages and each product page back to one or two useful guides.
- Update visible dates and Article `dateModified` only when content is genuinely reviewed.
- Keep claims factual and avoid fake urgency, fake discounts, invented reviews or unsupported reseller claims.

## 5. GEO and answer-engine optimization

- Maintain a consistent organization entity: name, logo, domain, WhatsApp, support hours, area served and policies.
- Keep `llms.txt` concise, factual and updated with the strongest canonical pages.
- Use clear question headings followed by direct, self-contained answers.
- Include definitions, comparisons, checklists, limitations and who each option is suitable for.
- Cite official product information when discussing features or official pricing in future articles.
- Publish original Pakistan-specific observations rather than rewriting supplier descriptions.
- Add author/editorial and review information, with a real review process.

## 6. Technical SEO

- Deploy the included `/blog/` hub and all sitemap URLs.
- Keep `robots.txt`, canonical URLs and HTTPS consistent.
- Submit `https://aitoolspak.tech/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console after deployment.
- Use URL Inspection for the home page, blog hub, three new commercial-intent guides and any heavily updated product page.
- Monitor Page Indexing, HTTPS, Core Web Vitals, Product snippets and manual actions.
- Run the included Node checks before every release.
- Do not index CSV, scripts, debug captures or internal reports.

## 8. Off-page and backlink plan

Prioritize relevance and legitimacy over volume.

- Create consistent profiles on genuine Pakistan business directories and startup/freelancer communities.
- Contribute useful answers and guides to university, freelancer, creator and small-business communities without spam.
- Pitch original Pakistan pricing data or comparison research to local technology blogs and newsletters.
- Offer guest articles about AI buying safety, creator workflows or student productivity to relevant sites.
- Ask real partners or customers with websites to mention the service where contextually relevant.
- Reclaim unlinked brand mentions and fix incorrect URLs.
- Avoid paid link farms, automated directory blasts, private blog networks and keyword-rich sitewide footer links.

Target the first 10–20 genuinely relevant referring domains before chasing high volume.

## 9. Google Business Profile

Create a Google Business Profile only if the business is eligible and can use a real, verifiable business location or service-area setup. Do not use a fake address. Keep business name, phone, website, hours and service categories consistent. Add real photos, publish updates, answer questions and request honest reviews from completed customers without incentives that violate platform rules.

## 10. Conversion and trust improvements

- Show final terms before payment: plan, duration, access type, price, delivery and support.
- Add a simple “last price checked” field to product pages.
- Add real order-support screenshots or testimonials only with permission and without exposing personal information.
- Create a comparison table for popular alternatives.
- Track WhatsApp clicks separately from page views.
- Avoid showing unverified stock or countdown timers.

## 11. Measurement dashboard

Track weekly:

- Search Console impressions, clicks, average position and CTR by query/page.
- Indexed versus submitted URLs.
- Non-brand organic clicks.
- Top 20 target-query positions.
- WhatsApp clicks and qualified conversations.
- Conversion rate from organic sessions to WhatsApp inquiry.
- New referring domains and linked pages.
- Content freshness and price-review status.

### 90-day directional targets

Use these as operating goals, not guarantees:

- All important canonical URLs indexed.
- 25–50% growth in non-brand impressions from the starting baseline.
- First-page movement for several long-tail Pakistan queries.
- 10+ relevant referring domains.
- Measurable WhatsApp inquiries attributed to organic landing pages.
