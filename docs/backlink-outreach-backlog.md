# Legitimate authority outreach backlog

This is a monthly operating backlog, not an automated link-building program.
Do not buy links, use PBNs, create fake reviews, or post irrelevant forum
links. Only publish or request a link where it is useful to the reader.

## Linkable site assets

1. Pakistan AI tools price index — keep PKR prices and plan durations current.
2. ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini for Pakistani students and freelancers.
3. AI subscription safety checklist for buyers who cannot pay with an
   international card.
4. AI tools for Pakistani freelancers and agencies.
5. AI API credits buying guide for local businesses.

## Monthly cadence

| Week | Action | Quality gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Update the price index and one comparison with verified plan data. | Do not publish unverified price, access, warranty or reseller claims. |
| 2 | Pitch 5 relevant Pakistani tech, freelancer, or creator publications with a useful original angle. | Relevance and editorial value first; no paid link schemes. |
| 3 | Submit accurate profiles to genuinely relevant business/tool directories. | Use real business identity and support contact details only. |
| 4 | Review Search Console queries and pages with impressions but weak CTR; update only when evidence supports it. | Do not claim Search Console access unless the owner supplies data. |

## Outreach record fields

Record the outlet, contact method, asset pitched, date, response, published
URL, and whether the placement is editorial. Never promise rankings or ask for
anchor-text manipulation.
