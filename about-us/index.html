About AI Tools Pak

Learn about AI Tools Pak, our WhatsApp order process, support hours and transparency rules for AI subscriptions in Pakistan.

What AI Tools Pak does

AI Tools Pak helps customers in Pakistan compare AI tool subscriptions, social media services, PKR prices, delivery notes and WhatsApp order support before payment. The goal is to make the buying decision clearer, not to hide plan details behind vague discount claims.

Who the service is for

The website is built for Pakistani students, freelancers, creators, agencies and small businesses that need paid AI access or social media service ordering with visible prices, support hours and buyer-safety reminders.

How listings are maintained

Product prices are stored in local catalog data and marked with last-verified dates on product pages. Social media service rates are imported from a priced PKR CSV catalog and are not converted again in the browser. Customers should still confirm availability and final order details on WhatsApp before payment.

Activation and support process

Orders start on WhatsApp. Support confirms the price, duration, access model, delivery estimate, refund condition and any usage limits before payment. Activation details are handled after confirmation, and customers should not share email passwords through the website.

Buyer safety principles

  • No fake official-partner claims.
  • No hidden prices when a verified catalog price exists.
  • No password fields on the website.
  • No fake reviews, ratings, founder profiles or social links.
  • Refund and delivery conditions are linked before ordering.
